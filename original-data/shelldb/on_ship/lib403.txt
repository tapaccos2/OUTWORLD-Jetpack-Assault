Dropship:
The TT-113 Gryphon dropship is the workhorse light transport used in the Colonial Defense Force. Capable of carrying an entire squad of marines and their equipment, the TT-113 is well armored and armed with a M-99 Pulse Blaster Cannon below the nose. The TT-113 Gryphon is a common, and welcome, sight for members of the Colonial Marines.
