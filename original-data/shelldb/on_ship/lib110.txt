Sergeant Alicia "La Diabla" Ramirez: 
Age: 23
Height: 5,4"
Weight: 120 lbs.
DOB: July 6, 2260

Background:
  Born on the planet Earth, Alicia is the third of five children born to Robert and Elena Ramirez. Her great grandfather was the Honduran freedom fighter Manuel Ramirez. Alicia's school records show her to have retained some of her great grandfather's renegade fighting spirit. Though she was never expelled, there are three occasions on which she was reprimanded for confronting a teacher.

  After graduating from high school, she joined the Colonial Defense Force as a means to leave Earth. During her first year in the corps, a confrontation with her CO nearly ended her military career; however, she was given second chance. 

  Alicia has since adapted to the structure of military life. She received a special citation for her combat skill during the Alstot incursion on Ramus III. Two years ago, she was accepted into the CDF Marine Jump Corps. At 22 years of age, her leadership skills and military professionalism won her the rank of sergeant.
