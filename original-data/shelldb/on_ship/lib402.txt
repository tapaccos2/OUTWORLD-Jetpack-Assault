CCS Ulysses: 
The CCS Ulysses is the newest, most powerful weapon in the CDF arsenal. The flagship of the CDF fleet, the Ulysses is twice as big as previous naval carriers. Carrying over 100 transports and fighter / strike spacecraft, the Ulysses leads Strike Force Omega in a constant, vigilant patrol throughout all the systems of the Colonial Commonwealth. The Ulysses is undisputedly the most powerful military ship in space, at least until its sister ship, the CCS Perecles, enters service in two years.
