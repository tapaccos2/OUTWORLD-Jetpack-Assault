Judd Kilgore

Age: 28
Height: 6'2"
Weight: 210 lbs.
DOB: December 10, 2255

Background:
  Judd Kilgore was born on the planet Arion to Dax and Rani Kilgore. Rani Kilgore died in a transport accident in 2258. Dax is recorded as working in several swing mining operations in various towns from the years 2258-2273, however, no records have been found of any permanent residence for either Dax or Judd during this time. Between the years of 2261 and 2263, there is no record or employ or residence for Dax, or record of enrollment in school for Judd.

  The sporadic scholastic records for Judd show him to have been quiet, never seeming to attach to any other children or any place. There are no records of disciplinary actions taken for or against him during his school years, and his performance was always steadily satisfactory.

  In 2273, there are unsubstantiated reports of criminal activity by Judd in the way of smuggling and bootlegging on the frontier. These reports continue through the year 2276 when Judd was apprehended by the Peripheral Guard and charged with a succession of trade violations.

  He was given the option of serving out his sentence in the military. He took this option and served in the 143rd infantry squad of the Outworld Expeditionary Guard. Offenders of this type are commonly assigned to this squad due to its high attrition rate.

  Judd excelled in the squad. His physical ability and "potential" were cited as reasons for his selection to the elite CDF Marine Jump Corps.

Combat Experience:
  Service in Outworld Expeditionary Guard 2276-2278
  CDF Marine Jump Corps 2278-present

Areas of Expertise:
  Trained in special ship to ground insert
  Certified in hand to hand, surface to air, and computer assisted combat
  Received honors in rocket pack air assault training
