Assassin:
Additional intelligence reports have classified no less than three different varieties of the "assassin" warrior class. Varying slightly in physical makeup, they are difficult to kill and use specialized weaponry. The most common variety fires a rapid-fire Hydran blaster. The smallest class of assassin is adapted for small, tight spaces, and usually wields a combination slow-firing blaster and "swarm gun". The largest of the three known classes wields a combination slow-firing blaster and grenade launcher.
