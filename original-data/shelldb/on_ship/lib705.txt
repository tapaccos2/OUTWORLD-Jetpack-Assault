Novus Prime:
Though not as verdant as Oasis, Novus Prime is indeed a planet of valuable resources. Her history is filled with battles between rival corporations fighting for the right to exploit her riches. Today the planet exists as a kind of commercial center, exporting huge quantities of foodstuffs to less hospitable planets such as Anubis. The ever-increasing population of Novus Prime may yet lead to the problems that once marred Earth. As for now, it exists as one of the more impressive and important planets within Colonial Space.
