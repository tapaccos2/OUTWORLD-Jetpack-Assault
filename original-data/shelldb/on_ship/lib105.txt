Captain John Russell

Age: 34
Height: 6'3"
Weight: 200 lbs.
DOB: February 7, 2249

Background:
  There are no birth records or records of any family for John Russell. The earliest personal records for John Russell are military recruiting records. 

  John Russell entered the military in 2269 as a private in the Oasis Protective Initiate. He advanced to the rank of Captain in 2272. Russell was Captain of the Oasis Cargo I Unit until 2273 when the entire crew disappeared during a routine flight between Oasis and Ramus III. 

  Russell reappeared on the planet Ragnarok in 2280. A post recovery interview identifies Russell as the sole survivor of the Oasis Cargo I Unit accident. Russell was released for extended recovery leave from 2280-2281. 

  Russell returned to duty in 2281 as a mission specialist in the CDF Marine Jump Corps.
