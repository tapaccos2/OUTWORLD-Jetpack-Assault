Berserker:
Encountered only rarely, the "berserker" warrior-class alien is among the most dangerous of the Hydran forces encountered so far. Equipped with a missile launcher and the lethal, short-range "spine gun," the Berserker is physically no bigger than the assassins, but is much more dangerous and almost impossible to kill. If encountered on the battlefield, the berserker is to be treated as a priority threat.
