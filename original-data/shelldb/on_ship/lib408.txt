Harbinger SAM:
Armored like a small tank and armed with a plentiful supply of AM-15ER missiles, the Harbinger Mobile Surface-to-Air Missile Launch Vehicle is capable of firing missiles to tremendous altitudes with great accuracy. It is a common sight just behind the lines of any battlefield, of any planet. Like the GT-3, the Harbinger is designed to be carried by the TT-113 Gryphon dropship.
