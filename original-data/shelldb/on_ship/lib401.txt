CCS Phoenix:
The CCS Phoenix is the remodeled, re-christened strike carrier that became the template for the vastly improved Ulysses class carrier. Capable of carrying up to 36 dropships and / or fighters, and a crew of over twelve hundred, the Phoenix began life as the CCS Andromeda. When the world of Novus Prime declared allegiance to the Colonial Commonwealth, the Andromeda successfully defended the planet against the corporate fleet blockading the planet. Following the engagement, the CCS Andromeda pulled into port at Oasis and underwent complete reconstruction. Two years later, the ship was rechristened the CCS Phoenix. Now, twenty years later, the Phoenix is still serving proudly in the Colonial Defense Force.
