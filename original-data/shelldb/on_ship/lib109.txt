Sergeant Henry "Bubba" Elkington: 
Age: 21
Height: 6'4"
Weight: 250 lbs.
DOB: February 3, 2262

Background:
  Henry was born on Oasis, the eldest of Abram and Eliza Elkington's six children. His parents had immigrated to Oasis as part of a new-age group, hoping to start a new life living close to the organic world as farmers. Henry's school records indicate that he was a hard worker, but that he lacked in certain areas of intelligence. 

  Henry's extra curricular activities included a daily regimen of boxing practice and weight lifting. He eventually won the colony's top boxing contest. His father planned for Henry to take over the family farm after he graduated from High School. However, he joined the Colonial Defense Force 4th division mid-way through his senior year in high school. 

  Henry's physical prowess and work ethic proved to be assets highly admired by his superiors. They noted that though he was not the smartest marine, he might prove to be one of the more valuable. After successfully participating in the battle of Timor, Henry was accepted into the elite CDF Marine Jump Corps. He has since proved to be more than equal to his tasks.

Military Experience:	
  Colonial Defense Force Expeditionary Guard 4th division 2273-2276
  CDF Marine Jump Corps 2282-present

Areas of Expertise:
  Trained in special ship to ground insertion and demolition
  Trained in Tactical Recovery of Spacecraft and Personnel (TRSP)
