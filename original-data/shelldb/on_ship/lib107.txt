Lance Corporal Markus "Hurley-Bird" Wendigo
Age: 22
Height: 6'3"
Weight: 190 lbs.
DOB: March 15, 2261

Background:
  Markus Wendigo was born on the planet Oasis. The middle child of an upper middle class family, Markus attended a very prestigious high school between the years 2275 and 2279, yet his education records show that he consistently received below average grades. However, these records also indicate that Markus received two awards for athletic competition during this period. 

  Arrested in 2281 for a series of practical jokes that resulted in 10,000 dollars worth of property damage, Markus was sentenced to one year's probation. Probationary records show that Markus was irresponsible and lacking in moral value.  

  During his probation, Markus entered flight school. Instructors describe Markus as "unreliable, a real wild card." In 2282, Markus was forced to leave the school after endangering two fellow students during an unscheduled flight. 

  After years of uncontrollable behavior, Markus' father, Judge John Wendigo, enrolled his son in the Marines in 2283 and called upon the assistance of his close friend, Jon Wallace, to assist him in getting Markus into the CDF Marine Jump Corps. 

Military Experience:	
  CDF Marine Jump Corps 2283 to present.

Areas of Expertise:	
  Completed rocket pack air assault training
