Hobbs Colony:
Hobbs colony is named after an eccentric man named Jacob Hobbs. A new age zealot, he had been run off of several planets for his radical teachings before he and his followers arrived at the barren planet of Anubis. To fund the colony, Hobbs made a deal with Draktar Mining Corp. to run their ferrobauxite extraction operation. Draktar executives jumped at the chance to hire the Hobberions since they were the first people who actually wanted to live on Anubis. Before Hobbs took over, their mining operations had failed miserably due to unreliable personnel. In contrast, Hobbs' zealous followers actually volunteered for hard labor, and the mining operation became profitable. In return, Jacob Hobbs and the Hobberions were free to practice their beliefs.
