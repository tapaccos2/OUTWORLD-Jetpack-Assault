Transport Craft:
The alien transport is a larger, slower ship that shares the starfish shape of the strike fighter. It is capable of carrying a sizeable contingent of aliens. Analysts estimate between twelve and forty-eight aliens are carried within the hold. The transport craft, like human Dreadnaut carrying dropships, rarely lands to deposit its passengers; it literally drops them at low altitudes and speeds. Reports indicate successful insertion of alien forces from altitudes as high as two hundred meters.
