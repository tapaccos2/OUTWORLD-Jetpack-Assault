Juggernaut:
The Juggernaut is the code-name for the giant alien spacecraft seen in the battle over Oasis. It apparently functions as both a combat craft and a seeder station. Large pods containing genetic material and chemicals are fired from the Juggernaut ship to a planet's surface where they immediately begin transforming the planet's climate to something presumably more suitable for the alien race. The genetic material acts as a kind of virus, causing spontaneous mutations in the cells of living creatures. The Juggernaut ship is extremely well defended, bristling with literally hundreds of energy and missile weapons. It moves by means of a directable plasma stream.
