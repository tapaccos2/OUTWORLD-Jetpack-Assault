Hydran Forces Intelligence:
In the last several weeks, many human colonies and outpost have come under attack by alien forces. Intelligence confirms that this was the prelude to a full-scale invasion by forces originating from a region of space commonly associated with the constellation Hydra. Since then, several of our colonies and remote outposts have fallen under attack. 

While much is unknown about the enemy, we have been able to gather some intelligence about their forces. They seem to be divided into several castes or classes that have specific duties. Even the "laborer" class has weaponry grafted to their bodies, indicating some flexibility of roles. The aliens also loose much of their cohesion when their chain-of-command is broken, indicating a lack of individual initiative and training. Local Hydran armies are controlled by a "queen." If the queen is destroyed, the Hydrans lose much of their organization and fighting effectiveness.

While the Hydran forces may seem strange and insect- or animal-like, do not be fooled into underestimating their intelligence. These aliens have advanced technology, and have displayed extreme cunning on the battlefield.
