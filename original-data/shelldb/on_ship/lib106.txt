Gunnery Sergeant Jonathan "Pappy" Wallace
Age: 42
Height: 6'1"
Weight: 190 lbs.
DOB: December 5, 2241

Background:
  Jonathan Wallace was born on the planet Oasis as the seventh son in a large family. Jon spent most of his youth on a farm in a rural area of Oasis.  His mother, Katherine Wallace, received her certificate in home schooling in 2245 and taught all her children at home. Jon married in 2259 and fathered three children. 

  Between 2261 and 2270, Jon maintained a farm on his home world, Oasis. In the year 2270, Oasis was the target of an unexpected enemy attack in a war between the colonies. Records documenting the damage of the attack list two of Jon's brothers as deceased and Jon's land was marked as taking the brunt of the hit. Stricken with grief, Jon swore he would dedicate the rest of his life to protecting his family. In 2271, Jon sold his land and joined the military in an effort to keep peace among the colonies.   

  "Pappy," as Jon is affectionately called, has gained the respect of his squad by providing the image of a father figure to his team. This characteristic becomes desperately important in the face of intense battle. 

  Jon is a smart fighter who has survived many battles. Although constantly being commended and recommended for higher positions, Jon has chosen to stay with the CDF Marine Jump Corps. His goal is to remain on the front lines and protect his team. He's more concerned with getting his soldiers out alive than becoming a hero. 

Military Experience: 	
  Front line infantryman 2271-2275 
  Special services commander 2275-2280  
  CDF Marine Jump Corps 2280-Present

Areas of Expertise:
  Received honors for leadership in a combat situation
  Trained in special ship to ground insertion and demolition
  Certified instructor of rocket pack assisted air assault
