Queens:
Hydran queens are invariably well protected. The strict hierarchical nature of the Hydran race makes the destruction of any known queens a top priority. In combat against Hydran forces at Anubis, destruction of a queen resulted in the almost immediate disintegration of the local Hydrans as a cohesive fighting force. It is impossible to characterize all queens based upon this one encounter; however, the queen was observed firing shock-guns and unless she was drawn out of her protective shell, she was nearly impossible to harm.
