1ST LIEUTENANT TWILA WRAY

Age: 33
Height: 5'8"
Weight: 133 lbs.
DOB: April 14, 2250

Background:
  Twila Wray was born on the planet Anubis to Jeffery and Lila Wray. Jeffery Wray was employed for 25 years in an underground mining operation on Anubis. He died in 2277. Lila Wray still remains on Anubis and is supported by her daughter Twila. 

  Education records present Twila as an average student with no significant achievements. She graduated from high school in 2268 and attended the World Academy of Science. She left the Academy in 2271, one year before graduation. There is no reason for dismissal or withdrawal in school records.

  Twila entered the military in 2272 as a private in the Marine Alpha Unit, testing water acidity and monitoring marine life in bodies of water throughout the planetary system. She received a planetary government award in 2276 for the discovery of life threatening acidity levels on the planet Ragnarok. 

  Wray served as a Corporal and Second Lieutenant in Strike Fleet Omega until 2281, when she transferred to the CDF Marine Jump Corps. She is an intelligent woman with exceptional leadership skills and a strong background in environmental science. 

Military Experience:
  Marine Alpha Unit 2272-2277
  Strike Fleet Omega 2277-2281
  CDF Marine Jump Corps 2281-present

Areas of Expertise:
  Received a special commendation for leadership in a combat situation
  Trained in special ship to ground insertion and demolition
