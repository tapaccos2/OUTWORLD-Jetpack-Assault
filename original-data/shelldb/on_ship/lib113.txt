Private Joey "Highball" Menunzo
Age: 19
Height: 5'11"
Weight: 180 lbs.
DOB: June 8, 2264

Background:
  Joey Menunzo spent his childhood quickly moving from colony to colony. After his parents filed for divorce in 2269, Joey became the target of an ugly custody battle. As a result, his mother, Mary Menunzo, attempted to avoid custody meetings and court dates by constantly moving. Spotty work records for Mary Menunzo and few school enrollment records for Joey make it impossible to establish residency between the years 2269 and 2277. 

  Between the years 2278 and 2281, Joey spent large periods of time at the Planetary Correctional Facility for Minors and was expelled from high school in 2280. The psychological affects of Joey's childhood seem to have attributed to this aggressive behavior. 

  Before being thrown out of school, he established a relationship with a young woman. The relationship has been unstable, but the two remain in contact, mostly due to their son, Joey Jr., born in 2280.

  Counseling records at the Planetary Correctional Facility indicate significant progress in Joey's psychological status. These records indicate Joey has worked through the psychological trauma of his childhood and is prepared to become a functioning member of society. 

  After being released from the correctional facility in 2281, Joey joined the military and found this very structured, high-adrenaline lifestyle to be the perfect outlet for his high-strung personality.
