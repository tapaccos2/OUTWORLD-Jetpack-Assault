Enemy Forces Intelligence:
On August 12, 2283, the deep-space outpost on Mikhal's world fell under attack by alien forces. Since then, several of our colonies and remote outposts have fallen under attack. While much is unknown about the enemy, we have been able to gather some intelligence about their forces. They seem to be divided into several castes or classes that have specific duties. Even the "laborer" class has weaponry grafted to their bodies, indicating some flexibility of roles. The aliens also loose much of their cohesion when their chain-of-command is broken, indicating a lack of individual initiative and training.

While the enemy forces may seem strange and insect- or animal-like, do not be fooled into underestimating their intelligence. These aliens have advanced technology and have displayed extreme cunning on the battlefield.
