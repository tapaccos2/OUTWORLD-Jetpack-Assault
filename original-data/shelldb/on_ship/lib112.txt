Age: 32

Background:
  Born on earth, Hans Dieter is of German decent. He was born into a stable family and had a very normal childhood. Between the years 2270 and 2275, Hans attended a prestigious college and graduated with honors. 

  Hans joined the FBI in 2276 and enjoyed a prosperous career until the FBI Character Research team uncovered an unexpected link in Hans' family. According to Character Research records, Hans' distant uncle was the notorious enemy spy Henrick Verhoven. Hans was immediately released from his position with the FBI. 

  After leaving the FBI in 2281, Hans joined the military. Recruiting forms list no relations or significant personal history. Hans declared to have learned his shooting skill from his favorite past time, hunting. This inaccurate record was clearly an attempt to hide the undesirable family history. However, due to the fact that Hans is a wonderful asset to his team and an excellent marksman, this error has been overlooked. He's cold-blooded, soft spoken, and an excellent marksman. A good man to have on your team in any situation.
