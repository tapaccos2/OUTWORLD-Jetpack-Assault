Staff Sergeant Emily "Snowflake" Williams:
Age: 27
Height: 5'8"
Weight: 132 lbs.
DOB: January 25, 2256

Background:
  Emily was born on the planet Novus Prime. Her parents are first generation colonists to the colony of New Britain. Her father is a doctor--a rather rare commodity on the colony. One of three children, Emily enjoyed a privileged childhood. Her grammar school days appear to be uneventful except for an alleged prank perpetrated by her that resulted in the embarrassment of a teacher. 

  She attended college at the New Oxford College on Novus Prime, majoring in biology. Though not having previously participated in organized sports, Emily joined the volleyball team and thrived in the atmosphere of intense competition. Emily has stated that biology was her parents choice for a major course of study, not hers. After being accepted into medical school, she surprised her parents and joined the Colonial Defense Force. 

  Her intellect allowed her to excel within the Marine Corp. She was promoted to private first class in the first year in the Corp. When her career path began to move in the direction of an office position, she asked to be transferred into the CDF Marine Jump Corps. Emily has again proved to be a quick study as a "Dreadnaut."

Military Experience:	
  Colonial Defense Force Marine Corp 2278-2281
  CDF Marine Jump Corps 2281-present

Areas of Expertise:
  Received honors for intelligence gathering in a combat situation
  Trained in special ship to ground insertion and demolition
