Jett Becker
Age: 23
Height: 5'9"
Weight: 135-140 lbs.
DOB: September 21, 2260

Background:
  Jett Becker was born on Ramus III, the only child born to Ian and Elena Becker. At age 6, she was enrolled in martial arts classes. She advanced quickly due to her agility and stamina and received a scholarship to the Ramus III Martial Arts Academy. Though Jett's scholastic records indicate superior aptitude and advanced placement, in 2276 her father removed her from the public school system. She was then placed in the employ of her father's architectural engineering firm. Jett remained there in an entry level drafting position until 2278, when she quit her job without notice and relocated to Anubis.

  Once in Anubis, Jett opened her own business; a hardware and mining supply company. The company fared well in its first fiscal term, but due to political pressure from the Draktar Mining Corporation, was forced out of business. Jett repeatedly attempted to renew the business, but the Anubis Board of Commerce denied seven charters for "unspecified reasons."

  In 2279, the Colonial Defense Force Expeditionary Guard recruited Jett Becker. Service records indicate exemplary performance, quick promotion, and a natural tendency toward leadership positions. Her skills, ambition, and intellect were cited as reasons for her recommendation to the CDF Marine Jump Corps.
