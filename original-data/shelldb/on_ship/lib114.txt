Lance Corporal Tyler "Cool Breeze" Miller
Age: 25
Height: 5'10"
Weight: 180 lbs.
DOB: October 8, 2258

Background:
  Tyler Miller was the only child born to Eva and Tyrone Miller. Tyler was born on the planet Novus Prime in a small upscale resort area and spent most of his youth in military boarding schools.

  Although his grades were strong, school records target Miller as somewhat rebellious. Between the years 2273 and 2275, Miller was reprimanded 12 times for breaking curfew and sneaking women into his living quarters after visiting hours. 

  Miller graduated from college in 2279. He declined two opportunities for civilian positions before joining the military in 2280. He is currently assigned to the CDF Marine Jump Corps. Miller earned the nickname "Cool Breeze" by remaining calm, even in the heat of battle. 

Military Experience: 	
  CDF Marine Jump Corps 2280-2283

Area of Expertise:	
  Certified combat electronics technician
  Trained in hand to hand, surface to air, computer assisted, and air to air combat.
