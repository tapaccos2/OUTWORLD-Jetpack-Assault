Picking your way through the crater will not be easy. You won't have a chance to rearm before your next mission, so you'll have to rely upon stealth and speed instead of firepower to get out of the crater. You'll also want to pick up whatever supplies you can find scattered across the terrain from the dropship's explosion.

The exit from the crater is blocked by a landslide, which can only be cleared by a high explosive, such as a timed-charge mine.

Be sure to protect Sgt. Ramirez from being killed by the enemy or from falling into the ravine.
