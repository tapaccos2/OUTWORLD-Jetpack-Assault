Your best bet is to make your way carefully to each of the five rock platforms surrounding the rail gun using the glider wing, then close your wings to reduce your detectability. Destroy the heat-sinks with the pulse rifle, since you need to save ordinance. If you attract enemy forces, destroy them quickly so the fighting doesn't attract more attention.

Once all five heat-sinks are destroyed, fly up to the top of the rail gun and enter the secondary vent. Don't worry about stealth at this point, just dodge fire!
