MISSION OVERVIEW

LOCATION: Anti-Orbital Installation, Ragnarok

Charlie team was unsuccessful in destroying the anti-orbital gun near your position.  It has a defensive shield above it, which combined with the danger of the gun itself and surrounding anti-aircraft weaponry, makes it impervious to aerial attack.

Remote scanning has determined the power source for the gun's shields is deep within a nearby crater.  If you can bring down the shields, it will be vulnerable. Destroy it with high explosives.
