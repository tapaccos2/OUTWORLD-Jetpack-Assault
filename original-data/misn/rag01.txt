MISSION OVERVIEW

LOCATION: Ragnarok LZ

We have rendezvoused with Strike Fleet Omega, in the system codenamed Ragnarok. This has been a staging area for numerous attacks on outlying colonial worlds, as well as the invasion of Oasis. It is of major strategic importance, acting as a beachhead for their invasion. A victory here will effectively cut their supply lines. Fierce fighting is expected, but based on Omega's success so far in turning the tide of invasion, we should be in good condition.

Alpha and Bravo teams will be providing rear security for our Marshal Point on the planet's surface. Don't stray too far from the designated perimeter, as the fleet will be raining holy terror from the skies in an all-out bombardment. Once they've softened the resistance, the goal is to annihilate their main base, which is partially underground and resistant to bombardment.   

Remember that Hydrans have the ability to burrow underground and hide beneath the planetary surface. The front-liners must not have their advance stalled by allowing Hydran assassins to slip past our defenses. 

Stand by for deployment.  Strike Fleet is about to enter the Ragnarok system.
