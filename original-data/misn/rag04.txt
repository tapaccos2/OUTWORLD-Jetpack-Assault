MISSION OVERVIEW

LOCATION: Interior, Ulysses Crash Site

We have received a message from Captain Russell, Intelligence Officer aboard the Ulysses.  He survived the crash and is trapped inside the wrecked ship.  Captain Russell has access to the complete cache of intelligence information gathered about the Juggernaut, the enemy supership that was used to destroy Oasis, as well as other vital information about enemy communications methods, tactics and weaponry.  Captain Russell and his information are too valuable to lose.

Proceed into the Ulysses and rendezvous with Captain Russell.  Get him and the intelligence files out of the ship.

Speed is critical.  The enemy is pouring forces into the area.  The Ulysses is becoming the eye of a firestorm.
