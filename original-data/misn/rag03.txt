MISSION OVERVIEW

LOCATION: Exterior, Ulysses Crash Site

The CDF flagship Ulysses was severely damaged by the energy beam weapon and crash-landed on the surface.  The ship is a loss, but we know for a fact that there are survivors and salvageable equipment and data. We're not going to leave them to the skulls.  We are sending in an assault force to sterilize the field of enemy presence so we can begin a rescue & recovery operation.

You're team will be spearheading the assault. You will have aerial and armored support. Make sure there isn't a single living Hydran within two klicks of the Ulysses when you are done.
