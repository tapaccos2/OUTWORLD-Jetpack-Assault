MISSION OVERVIEW

LOCATION: Oasis Military Research Facility

Technicians have been developing an attachment for the standard combat suit, which, in conjunction with the rocket-packs, would essentially turn each infantryman into a self-contained glider.  It's not necessary to explain how useful this would be, or how we don't have the time to duplicate all the work spent to design it.  The finished prototypes are in the Research Facility.  You are to infiltrate the facility, locate the glider pod prototypes, and get them to the roof to be picked up. To activate the freight elevator, stand on the pressure plate located immediately inside and to the right of the sliding doors.

Alien forces have infiltrated the entire area.  Our laboratories and intelligence centers seem to be favorite targets, so expect them to be there ahead of you.  Get in and out as quickly as you can.  The evacuation of the planet is ongoing.
