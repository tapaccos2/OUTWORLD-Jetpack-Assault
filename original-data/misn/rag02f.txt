That gun MUST be destroyed by any means necessary. You have to move past it to the pit on the opposite end of the crater and destroy the generator located there. It will probably be impervious to small-arms fire, so you'll have to use more powerful weaponry. 

Once it is down, the gun will be vulnerable. Return to the gun and destroy it with remaining high-explosive mines. 

There is no way to re-supply you.  It may be gruesome, but look for fallen teammates and scavenge their weapons and ammunition.  This is no time to be squeamish.
