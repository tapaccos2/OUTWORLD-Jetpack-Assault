MISSION OVERVIEW

LOCATION: Interior, Ulysses Crash Site

The demo charges weren't sufficient to scuttle the Ulysses. We think the skulls were able to remove many of the charges before they blew. Whatever the reason, we have a wreckage down there that still contains very sensitive data and technology. The Hydran forces are converging on the area as we speak, but we still have one last chance to scuttle the ship - and wipe out a few hundred Hydrans in the bargain.

We are sending you back into the Ulysses. Make your way to the engine room and activate another demolition charge.  Then get far away fast.  Once the demo charge goes, the engine will follow suit with the power of a small sun. There won't be anything left of the ship - or most of the valley - within about five minutes.
