MISSION OVERVIEW

LOCATION: Alien Vessel, Codenamed Juggernaut

Intelligence successfully interrogated the prisoner. The Mastermind is apparently bred to receive and transmit information. It reacted to our orders to talk by telling everything it knew. We now have the location of both the Juggernaut reactor core and the lair of the alien Queen. It also gave some indication of the arrival of another even more serious threat, but that will have to be dealt with later.

There will be a two-pronged attack, each serving as a diversion for the other.  The engineering team will attempt to mine the reactors. You are to lead an assault on the Queen.  

The deep interior of the Juggernaut is wide open. There is no way to cross it without giving yourselves away, so move as fast as possible to avoid an attack by enemy forces. The first step is to use the glider wings to provide close air support to the engineers as they head for the reactor. Stay close and protect them until they make it to cover.

This is going to wake them up. Once you hit the open, don't slow down.
