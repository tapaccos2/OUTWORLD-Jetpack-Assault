MISSION OVERVIEW

Our next step is to insert you into the planetary rail gun. This is not going to be easy. With only one Inertial Dampening Unit, you'll be flying solo. We'll be setting up a diversion about twenty kilometers east of you, to help draw off defenders.

We're outfitting you with glider wings for this drop. You will need to evade enemy air and ground patrols on your way to the entrance of the enemy rail gun. The rail gun doors are closed, but we think we can force this clam to open up. By destroying the five heat-sinks around the main entry port, you should force a secondary vent to open at the top of the rail gun structure.

Be careful and try to avoid any patrols. You will not be returning to the ship until the rail gun is destroyed and the Hydrans are stopped.
