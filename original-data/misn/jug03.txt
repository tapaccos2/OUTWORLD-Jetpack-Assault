MISSION OVERVIEW

LOCATION: Alien Vessel, Codenamed Juggernaut

The engineering team is homing in on the reactor core. Your attack on the Queen will hopefully confuse the enemy. Plus, destruction of the Queen will throw the alien forces into disarray. It might leave the Juggernaut crippled even if the engineers fail.

The enemy is alerted to your presence. The Queen's chamber is located along your return path to the hangars. By now, it will be the most heavily defended spot on the Juggernaut and all enemy forces are on high alert. Fly over there and overthrow her majesty, then return for immediate departure.

Good luck. Make us proud.
