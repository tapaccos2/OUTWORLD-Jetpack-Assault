MISSION OVERVIEW

LOCATION: Alien Vessel, Codenamed Juggernaut

When we destroyed the Queen on Anubis, the Hydran forces fell into disarray almost immediately. When we attacked at Ragnarok, the Hydrans concentrated their attack on the Ulysses, rather than the incoming transports. It was as if they expected the destruction of our flagship would have the same effect on us as the destruction of a Queen would have on them. They live by a rigid hierarchy, and disruption of that hierarchy throws them into chaos for a time. We expect that the giant "Juggernaut" starship is, or holds, a Hydran Queen. If we can take it out, we expect to see their forces fall into disarray. We will use this time to reestablish a foothold in the Oasis system and slowly recapture the planet as we get reinforcements from Earth.

Your team's mission is to infiltrate the alien "Juggernaut." We are gambling that the stolen Hydran spacecraft will get past any security measures with a minimum of scrutiny. From the looks of it, most of the forces that would normally be on board the ship have moved to the planet's surface. The hierarchical nature of the alien society seems to indicate that everybody sticks to his own job - no one should notice your ship as long as you don't do anything to attract attention to yourself.

Your first job after entering the Juggernaut will be to breach the enemy's first level of internal security and capture a "Mastermind" class alien. You will have to strike very carefully, but very quickly. If even one of them manages to raise the alarm, the entire mission is over. You will go solo, in a scout suit, to minimize the possibility of detection. Use the new Gauss Rifle attachment to pick off guards - it is as silent as it is deadly. If you run out of ammo, come back to the ship. We'll have some reloads waiting for you. 

When you find the Mastermind, destroy any guards or security escorts that will probably be protecting it and bring the Mastermind back alive. Wound it if necessary, but DO NOT KILL IT. We know enough about the alien language and physiology to get invaluable information from it.

Remember, watch the alarm platforms. You must prevent the enemy command from learning of your presence for as long as possible. An alarm at this point would be fatal.
