MISSION OVERVIEW

LOCATION: Oasis Military Research Facility

The Hydrans have been extremely busy - they have turned the planet into a giant rail gun. A huge tunnel passes through the very center of the planet, powered by the thermal energy from the planet's core.

The rail gun structure on the planet seems linked with a geosynchronous space station. The station is a flying fortress and weapon emplacements heavily defend the structure on the surface. With our limited forces in this system, we don't dare try a frontal assault on the rail gun or the space station. Our best chance for a successful attack is a commando infiltration of the enemy installation. You have been chosen for the job.

The rail gun must be destroyed from within. Unfortunately, it is capable of generating forces that would tear an armored human to shreds in seconds. However, there is one possible maneuver that may help you survive this mission. Besides the glider-wing pods, researchers on the planet Oasis were working on an Inertial Dampening Unit that would enable an armored jump-trooper to survive high-gravity environments and high-speed drops. Only one prototype was built and never fully tested, but if it works, it will allow survival within the active rail gun long enough to destroy it.

We don't know if the prototype is still in the lab, and we don't know if it will work, but it's our only chance.

You have to go in and get it. We strongly recommend the scout suit - with the electricity turned off in the base, you will need all the rocket-pack capability you can get to fly up the elevator shafts. Should you come under attack, we strongly recommend against pressing an attack - just get out alive.
