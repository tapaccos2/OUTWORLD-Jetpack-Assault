MISSION OVERVIEW

LOCATION: Exterior, Ulysses Crash Site

Salvage and rescue teams are trying to finish work on the Ulysses. A new wave of alien ground and air forces are arriving and are threatening the salvage teams. 

The decision has been made:  We are not leaving any personnel or sensitive material on that ship for the enemy to capture.  A sapper team is onboard planting demolition charges that will set off the ship's fusion torpedoes.

Your team has a difficult mission. You must escort the last two trucks to their waiting dropships and prevent the destruction of the two mobile SAM launchers that will be providing protection from enemy transports and strike fighters. Each of these vehicles are considered top priority - we recommend splitting up your team and setting them as guards.

Hold out until at least one of the incoming enemy ships is shot down to complete the mission.
