MISSION OVERVIEW

LOCATION: West of Anubis Mines

We are going to take the fight to the enemy this time, but on our terms. Our plan is to unseal the mine entrance and strike from there. Attempts to breach the mine entrance from the air have been unsuccessful. High explosive bombs are unworkable due to risk of collapsing the tunnel. We need a surgical strike to infiltrate the mines, engage the enemy, and look for surviving colonists.

The mine entrance is located in a crater, which is heavily defended. Strike craft and assault teams are being driven back by missile-firing defenders and enemy flyers. We need something as small and maneuverable as the flyers to break in and take out their defenses. 

Your team is to use the new glider-wing pods to fly to the tunnel entrance. There is no time for training missions; you have to learn on the job. Neutralize enemy flyers and all anti-aircraft threats around the mine entrance. Once the area is clear, a strike ship will destroy the seal.
