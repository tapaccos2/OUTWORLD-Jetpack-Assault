The projectile that is headed for the rail gun appears to be a giant seedpod, capable of transforming a planet all by itself, like Juggernaut transformed Oasis. In addition, it contains the cultural history and collective mind of the Hydran race. 

As far as we can tell, this is the final stage of a complete relocation of their species into our space. Should this thing be allowed to hit, all life on the target planet would be eliminated immediately and we'd be facing the mother of all Hydran Queens. We don't have the forces to fight the Hydrans should this happen. However, if we take out this "brainseed" now, our job is all but over. Should we fail, we don't have the forces remaining to defend ourselves. We'll be swarmed and made extinct. It's that black and white.

Prepare to plunge.
