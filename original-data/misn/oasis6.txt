MISSION OVERVIEW

LOCATION: Oasis Command Center / Reactor Core

The alien landing force is too strong to resist.  They are overrunning the planet.  

The evacuation is proceeding in as orderly a manner as possible, but valuable technology and unsecured information have been left behind in the Command Installation.  The aliens have already occupied the installation.  We cannot allow data to be used against us later.

We must scuttle the base.  Nothing of use to the enemy can remain intact. You and your team are the only unit available-other teams are escorting evacuation vehicles.  You will go into the Command Center and destroy the three cooling regulators. This will destroy the main coupling, and cause the generator to go critical. You must escape before the generator explodes.

Time is scarce.  Your success is crucial.
