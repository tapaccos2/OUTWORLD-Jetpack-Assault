MISSION OVERVIEW

Location: Hobb's Mining Colony, Anubis.

The miners on Hobb's Colony discovered what appeared to be a very old crash site of an alien spacecraft.  Shortly thereafter, there was an alien attack.  At this point we don't know where the attack originated.  We have had no further communications from Hobb's Colony since the final distress call.

There is very little intelligence as to the exact number or physical nature of the hostiles, except that there was apparently a sizable force capable of overwhelming the colony's defensive perimeter.  Condition of surviving colonists, if any, is also unknown.   

Your mission is search and rescue.  Locate any civilians by telemetry from their subdermal locator beacon implants and move them to a secured area for evacuation.  Engage and destroy any alien forces you encounter.
