Our secondary forces have secured the colony.  We have yet to account for all the missing colonists.

Colonists' logs indicate that miners disturbed a hidden underground alien base, apparently left here after the crash of a very large alien spacecraft.  The alien force was dormant, in a deep hibernation, but became active when disturbed and attacked the humans.  Colonists managed to seal off the entrance to the mine, but the enemy forces found another way out.  

We have located a series of strange hills or "silos" east of the colony which seem to be of recent origin.  Though we have no direct evidence yet, we strongly suspect that they are connected with the underground alien complex.

Reconnaissance is ongoing in that sector.
