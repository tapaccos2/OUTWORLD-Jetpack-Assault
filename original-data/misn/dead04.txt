MISSION OVERVIEW

Location:  Inside the Hydran Rail Gun

We have calculated the arrival time of this "Brainseed" as accurately as possible, based on its current speed and location. There is not much time. We have also calculated the orientation of the rail gun and all possible targets. There is no mistake. The Brainseed is being aimed at the Sol system - right at Earth. If we fail, billions of lives will be snuffed out in a matter of days, replaced by a swarming hive of Hydrans.

Scanners have isolated what seems to be the control center, or "core," of the rail gun. You will need to descend deeply through the "barrel" of the railgun to locate the core, then drop in and sabotage the control center. We have no idea what sort of defenses you may encounter. We can only hope that they haven't counted on this sort of assault and won't have planned for it. We've sent in some dropships to air-drop supplies down the shaft. Hopefully some will be accessible for you.

This will be a lot like scuttling the Ulysses. You will need to get within a few feet of what intelligence believes to be a control console directly connected to the main power generator and then drop one of your special explosive charges. The charge is on a proximity fuse and will detonate when the Brainseed arrives. This should assure that there is no chance of missing. We won't get another shot. There should be ample time to escape the rail gun. Please make certain that the area is clear of any Hydran who may be capable of disarming the explosive device.

After you have set the charge, drop down through the tunnel. The Inertial Dampening Unit will protect you from the g-forces and you will be ejected out the other end of the gun. A rescue ship will catch up with you and pick you up.

The survival of Earth depends on this mission. You must not fail.
