MISSION OVERVIEW

LOCATION: Phoenix Main Deck

Intelligence missed on this one. There is some sort of space station in orbit above the planet. Enemy orbital strength is much greater than anticipated.

Hydran forces have launched a kamikaze attack. They have breached the mothership's hull aft of the command center and successfully boarded a party of unknown strength and composition. The rear third of the ship has suffered rapid decompression. The enemy seems to be headed toward the ship's engine compartments. It looks like they're trying to pull the same thing we did to them on Juggernaut, only without the subtle entrance.

Get moving. Keep them out of the engine compartment at all costs.
