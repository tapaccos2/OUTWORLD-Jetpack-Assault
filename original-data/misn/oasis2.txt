MISSION OVERVIEW

LOCATION: Training Area B

This is a mobility and target acquisition exercise.  The goal is to jump to the various new points while taking out the targets along the way.  You will be given a combat suit, which holds more weaponry than the scout suit you wore in your last exercise. You will need them. This is a timed exercise, so you must complete the mission before the clock runs out. You may complete the objectives in whatever order you see fit.

And don't make us have to fish you out of the drink.
