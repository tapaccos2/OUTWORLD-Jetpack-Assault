Several waves of enemy flyers will need to be eliminated, but do not ignore the defenders on the ground. They are equipped with missile launchers and can be as deadly to you - and the strike craft - as the flyers. 

Try to fly below the pinnacles to avoid missile fire from the defenders and use your speed to spoil the gun turret shots. Homing missiles are most effective against the flyers and you can use proximity mines to "dive bomb" ground-based threats.
