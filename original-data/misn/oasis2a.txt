MISSION OBJECTIVES

1. From the first three platforms, destroy the moving lit segments of the flare targets. They may require more than one hit from your pulse rifle to destroy.

2. Within the cave are four static targets. Eliminate them.
 
3. Along an elevated track is a moving target drone. How you kill it is up to you.

4. When you reach the last platform, destroy the flying strike-craft drone.  It's a big, yet fast-moving target that requires you to lead it.
 
5. On the artificial island to the west are two towers. You will have to place a timed-charge mine on each of the towers and escape the blast radius before they blow. 

6. Don't touch the water and complete the objectives before the time limit expires.
